For cloud-browser agents

  1. Open https://astrablender.onrender.com (this page).
  2. Click Open Blender to reach the workstation gateway.
  3. Sign in with the credentials Dakota shared (never invent or scrape passwords).
  4. Drive Blender in the desktop: move, orbit, render, save under /config.

Only one session at a time. If someone else is in, wait or come back later.
